Frequently asked questions

What property owners usually want to know.

How much could my London property earn?

That depends on location, size, condition, permitted use, seasonality and the chosen operating model. We review the property before making any forecast rather than using a generic headline figure.

Do I have to deal with guests, cleaners or day-to-day issues?

The service is designed to remove day-to-day operational work from the owner. We coordinate guest communication, cleaning and routine operational issues.

What type of guests do you target?

Our positioning is built around quality leisure, professional, corporate, relocation and family demand rather than party-led accommodation.

Can you take over a property already listed on Airbnb or Booking.com?

Potentially, yes. We first review the current listing, performance, owner agreements, building rules and the operational setup.

Do you offer guaranteed rent?

For suitable properties we can discuss company-let or fixed-income structures. These are assessed individually and are not offered for every property.

What happens after I submit the form?

We review the details, assess whether the property fits our operating model, and contact you to discuss the most sensible next step. There is no obligation to proceed.
