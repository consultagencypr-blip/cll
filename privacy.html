Privacy notice

Last updated: 22 September 2026.

This notice explains how information submitted through this website's landlord and property-owner enquiry form is used.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, email address, telephone number, property postcode or area, property type, bedroom count, current property status, your stated management goals, and campaign or referral information attached to the enquiry.

Why we use it

We use this information to assess your property enquiry, contact you about relevant property-management services, prepare proposals where appropriate, and understand which marketing channels generate enquiries.

Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers used to host the website, store form submissions, manage customer enquiries, or deliver related business systems. We do not publish your enquiry information.

Retention and your rights

We keep enquiry information only for as long as reasonably needed for the business purpose, legal requirements, or legitimate record keeping. You may ask for access, correction, or deletion of your information, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the business using the contact details provided to you during your enquiry. Before publishing paid campaigns, add the confirmed legal entity name and a dedicated privacy email address here.
